***Horsey Jockey***

*"Welcome to a town called Horseyland, where are plentiful of Horseys around town's plains.
You came to this town and bought old ranch, with the goal to get rich and be famous.
Your adventure begins here, dear friend."*

*Horsey Jockey* is simulation game with no goal and with no end, programmed in Java, where
you will be in boots of new jockey in town full of Horseys, catch them, feed them and race with them .

**Mechanics**

Player attributes:
* Money - you will need to control your wealth, because nothing is free
* Food - another attribute you will need to watch over, don't feed your Horsey and it will die
* Lasso - needed for catching other Horseys, when the last one died

Game systems:
* Days - each time another day comes prices changes and quantity replenishes
* Death - as stated before, your Horsey can die, you can either bury it and pay respects or sold it's dead body for quick cash, choice is on you
* Strength - Horsey's attribute that changes a lot of actions, be it too weak you will be losing money and Horseys, higher the number, better the outcome
* Market - prices are dynamic and changes each day, and limited. Be sure you have enough of each thing, otherwise you won't be able to buy it next day
* Arena - place, where you can bet on your Horsey and will big, be aware tho, betting is expensive and sometimes you can lose everything instantly. Another is Horse's hunger, be it hungry and it will die.
